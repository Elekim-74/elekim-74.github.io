Hermes Personal Boards

This is the public information page for a personal connector used by its owner. It lets the owner's AI assistant, Hermes, read and write two shared Google Docs boards and, at the owner's direction, work with Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts on a dedicated Google account.

The connector is intended for the owner's personal use, not as a public service. This page provides information for Google's consent screen.
