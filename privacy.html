Privacy Policy - Hermes Personal Boards

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Purpose and access

With the owner's authorisation, the connector can access Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts on the owner's dedicated account. Its intended purpose is to carry out the owner's tasks, chiefly reading and posting updates on two shared Google Docs.

Processing

Google user data is processed by Google and the AI service providers Hermes uses. Those providers may retain processed content under their own terms; this policy makes no promises about their retention practices.

Commitments

The owner does not sell or rent personal data and does not use Google user data for advertising.

Storage

The connector's Google sign-in token is stored on the owner's computer. Board updates remain in Google Drive.

Changes and contact

Policy changes will appear on this page. Contact is through the support email displayed on the app's Google consent screen.
